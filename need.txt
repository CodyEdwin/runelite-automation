GE FLIP ADVISOR - jar rebuilt 16:19
==================================

This is now an advisor. It does not click, buy, sell or type anything. It
watches the OSRS Wiki price feed, works out which items can be bought low and
resold high *after* the 2% Grand Exchange tax, and shows you the exact
numbers. You place every offer yourself.


HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
RuneLite sidebar -> "RSBuddy Scripts" -> choose "GE Flipper" in the script
dropdown -> Start.

A window called "GE Flip Advisor" opens (always on top). If you close it,
press Start again to bring it back. If you are not logged in yet the status
line says "Waiting for login"; prices start loading as soon as you are in
game.


WHAT TO DO WITH IT
------------------
1. "Starting capital (gp)" - type what you actually have. The window will not
   suggest anything you cannot afford, and it sizes each buy to that amount.

2. "Free to play only" - ticked by default. Leave it ticked and member items
   are hidden. Untick it only if you want to see member trades.

   This box re-scans the moment you change it; you do not need Apply.

3. The orange card is the pick. It reads like this:

       Buy 125 @ 3,403 gp  ->  list them at 3,614 gp

   Those are exact prices. Type those exact numbers into the GE. The line
   under it shows total profit after tax, total cost, 24h volume, buy limit,
   the break-even price and "never list below".

4. "Copy name" puts the item name on your clipboard so you can paste it into
   the GE search box. "Next pick" cycles through the other candidates.

5. Buy them, wait for the buy to fill, then sell them at the sell price. Do
   not list below the "never list below" number - that is the price that
   still covers what you paid plus tax plus your minimum profit.

6. "Apply filters & rescan" after changing capital, min profit or anything in
   the advanced section. The list also refreshes itself every few seconds.


READING THE LIST
----------------
"All picks" is sorted by total profit, biggest first. Columns:

    Item   Buy   Qty   Sell   Profit   ROI   Vol 24h   Limit

Buy and Sell are the exact prices to use. Qty is how many the window thinks
you can move: capped by your capital, the item's 4-hour buy limit, and 10% of
its daily traded volume so you are not left holding a pile nobody buys.


ADVANCED FILTERS (hidden by default)
------------------------------------
Min ROI %          - profit as a percentage of the buy price
Min 24h volume     - ignore items that barely trade
Buy offset         - how far above the current low you offer (default +1)
Sell offset        - how far below the current high you list (default -1)
Max 24h price drift- reject items whose price has moved too far from its
                     24-hour average, which is how spikes and crashes get
                     filtered out

Defaults are deliberately picky: at least 50 gp profit per item, 1% ROI, 250
items traded per day, and no more than 8% drift from the 24h average.


IF IT SAYS "No pick right now"
------------------------------
That is not an error. It means nothing passed every filter at that moment.
Usually the market is quiet, or your capital is too small for the items with
real volume. Try, in this order:

    1. Lower "Min profit per item" to 20-30 and press Apply.
    2. Lower "Min 24h volume" to 100.
    3. Raise "Max 24h price drift" to 12-15%.
    4. Wait a few minutes; the feed refreshes constantly.


THE HONEST CAVEATS
------------------
- Prices come from the OSRS Wiki, not from your client. They are recent, not
  live: the low and high you see already happened. That is why the window
  rejects quotes older than 30 minutes and prices far from the daily average.
- A flip is only a plan. If the market moves between the scan and your offer,
  you may buy and then have to wait, or sell at break-even. The "never list
  below" number is what stops a bad sale from turning into a loss.
- Tax is always assumed: 2% of the sale price, rounded down, capped at 5m per
  item. A few items are exempt in game; assuming tax can only make the
  estimate more cautious, never less.
- The buy limit is per 4 hours, per item. The quantity already respects it.
- Nothing here can guarantee a profit. It cannot see other players' offers,
  only what has already traded.


WHERE THE LOGS GO
-----------------
Every line the window logs is also written to:

    %USERPROFILE%\.runelite\rsbuddy-flipper.log

If something looks wrong, just say so - I read that file directly, you do not
need to copy anything.
